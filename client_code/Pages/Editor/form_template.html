📚 Sources the secret ingredient

Link the original study, dataset or expert page, not only news coverage of it. A DOI link (https://doi.org/…) is perfect. Well-sourced articles are much more likely to be approved, and readers trust them more.

🥼 Lab style guide
  • Cute is great, misleading is not. Don't exaggerate what was found.
  • “In mice” matters. Say who or what was studied, and how many.
  • Say how sure scientists are. That's what the evidence level is for.
  • Credit the researchers and link their work.
